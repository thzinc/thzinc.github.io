After multiple years of bike commuting with a hearty Topeak iPhone case and bike mount, I found I needed to switch phone mounts in order to find a system that worked with both my bikes, and also my desk phone mount. Quad Lock has a nice system and iPhone cases that explicitly support wireless charging, so I got the [MAG Wireless Charging Head](https://www.quadlockcase.com/collections/mag/products/mag-wireless-charging-head) to mount at my desk.

However, my desk has a typical 1/4" tripod mounting screw and the Quad Lock system uses metric M5 bolts, so I decided to make a small adapter. The design uses a 1/4" nut and an M5 heat set insert to secure each of the screws. The adapter is ~33mm in diameter, has a raised ridge to fit in the Quad Lock's mounting groove, and a simple hexagonal cut out to hold the 1/4" nut without having to print overhangs.

![3D rendering of adapter as described](/assets/quad-lock-adapter/render.png)

Sent it to the printer and about an hour later, I had my adapter!
