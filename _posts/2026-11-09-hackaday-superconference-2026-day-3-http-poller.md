While thinking about what I could do with the Billboard Badge, so many of my thoughts tended toward showing something via image that was downloaded from the internet. I initially started making a utility for myself, but decided to contribute a user app I called "HTTP Poller" to periodically wake up, connect to via WiFi, and download an image from a URL.

My demo was underwhelming, but I hope others who wanted to do the same benefit from a useful starting point.
